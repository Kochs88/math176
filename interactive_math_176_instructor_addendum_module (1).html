MATH 176: Instructor Addendum Interactive Module

Welcome to your interactive guide for Business Calculus (MATH 176). This module explains the course structure, grading policies, schedule, expectations, assignments, and important success strategies for Summer II 2026.

Course Overview

This course introduces students to Business Calculus Applications, focusing on limits, derivatives, optimization, and integration with real-world business applications.

📍 Meeting Information

Days: Monday & Wednesday

Time: 9:00 AM – 11:50 AM

Location: MAS 210

📚 Course Modality

This is an in-person course with online videos and online homework integrated into instruction.

🧠 Main Topics

  • Limits & Continuity
  • Derivatives
  • Optimization
  • Antiderivatives & Integrals
  • Business Applications

What Makes This Course Unique?

Business Calculus connects mathematics to economics, business decision-making, revenue analysis, cost modeling, optimization, consumer surplus, and more.

What technology will I use?

Students will use Canvas for homework, videos, projects, announcements, and accessing the free ebook.

What support resources are available?

Free tutoring is available through the Math Lab in MAS 204 and through remote tutoring services.

Course Timeline & Schedule

The course is fast-paced because Summer II is condensed. Staying current with assignments is essential.

June 1 – June 17

Algebra review, limits, continuity, and introductory derivative concepts. Test 1 on June 17

June 22 – July 13

Derivative formulas, chain rule, exponential functions, maxima/minima, optimization. Test 2 on July 13

July 15 – August 5

Antiderivatives, definite integrals, area under curves, consumer surplus, income distribution. Test 3 on August 5

📝 Exams

All three exams must be completed in person during class.

📌 Quizzes

Quizzes cannot be made up. The lowest quiz grade is dropped.

💻 Homework

Homework is completed online through Canvas and includes uploading handwritten work.

📂 Projects

Two projects must be submitted electronically as PDF files.

Important Academic Policies

  • No late homework assignments are accepted.
  • No late projects are accepted.
  • No curves are provided on exams.
  • A grade of C or better is required for prerequisite purposes.
  • Students are expected to follow academic honesty policies.

Strategies for Success

⏰ Stay Organized

Summer courses move quickly. Use a calendar to track homework, quizzes, and exam dates.

📖 Practice Daily

Calculus is learned through consistent practice. Work on problems every day.

🤝 Use Tutoring Resources

Visit the Math Lab early if you feel confused. Getting help early is the key to success.

🧩 Review Algebra

Strong algebra skills are essential for success in calculus.

Common Student Misconceptions

Misconception Reality
“Calculus is only about memorizing formulas.” Calculus focuses on understanding change, rates, and applications.
“I can fall behind and catch up later.” Summer courses move too quickly for long delays.
“Homework is optional practice.” Homework directly supports quiz and exam success.
